# Financial Planning as a Retiree

In retirement your have three elements of financial planning to focus on.  Your budget, your income, and your assets.

## Budget

I will provide tools to help with the budget.  List of elements of a retirees budget.

- Housing
- Utilities
- Transportation
- Health, Dental, Vision Insurance and Expenses
_ Food
- Entertainment
- Clothing
- Repairs

## Income

Income sources for retirees include pensions and Social Security.  Other sources of income - interest, dividends, rental, work
